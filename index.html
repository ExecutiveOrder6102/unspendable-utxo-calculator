Bitcoin DCA Fee Calculator

See how small frequent on-chain bitcoin purchases could become expensive to spend in the future

Who is this tool for?

This is designed to help new users understand the implicaitons of buying on-chain. Unfortunately people can be driven by high-time preference behaviour and decide to move/sell all their bitcoin when everybody else is thinking the same thing! DCA (Dollar Cost Averaging) is a strategy that helps you buy bitcoin over time, reducing the impact of price volatility but also it can be your own worst enemy if you don't understand the implications.

Assumptions made by this tool

  • You're buying BTC on an exchange that immediately sends you the BTC after purchase.
  • That you are receiving the full amount of BTC that you paid for, and not including any exchange withdrawal fees.
  • That you are using a "native segwit" wallet that addresses start with bc1p (P2WPKH - Pay to Witness Public Key Hash).
  • That the price will remain the same during your DCA period, this won't be true but use it as a rough guide.
  • That you do not perform any UTXO management, and will want to move all of your BTC in one go.

What are UTXOs?

Every time you buy bitcoin, it creates a separate piece of bitcoin (called a UTXO) in your wallet. When you want to spend your bitcoin later, you need to pay a network fee for each UTXO you use. If the network fees are high and you have many small UTXOs, it might cost more in fees than your bitcoin is worth!

What do I do with this information?

Explore different options available to you, for example if you're on a smaller budget:

  • Buy more bitcoin, but less frequently
  • Use a custodial exchange, wait till you have a larger amount (in satoshis) before withdrawing
  • Use an exchange that interoperates with the lightning network for moving smaller amounts

You should read up on the Bitcoin Transaction Fees and UTXO Management - Matt Odell understand further on what UTXO sizes are "good" as a baseline, and how you can "consolidate" them to save on fees in the future

You should also investigate different kinds of wallets, and especially if you want to consider a multi-sig wallet in the future then UTXO sizes are an incredibly important consideration Bitcoin Address Types Compared - Unchained Capital
