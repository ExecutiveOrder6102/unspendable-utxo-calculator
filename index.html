Who is this tool for?

This is designed to help new users understand the implicaitons of buying on-chain. Unfortunately people can be driven by high-time preference behaviour and decide to move/sell all their bitcoin when everybody else is thinking the same thing! DCA (Dollar Cost Averaging) is a strategy that helps you buy bitcoin over time, reducing the impact of price volatility but also it can be your own worst enemy if you don't understand the implications.

Assumptions made by this tool

- You're buying BTC on an exchange that immediately sends you the BTC after purchase.

- That you are recieving the full amount of BTC that you paid for, and not including any exchange withdrawal fees.

- That you are using a "native segwit" wallet that addresses start with bc1p (P2WPKH - Pay to Witness Public Key Hash).

- That the price will remain the same during your DCA period, this won't be true but use it as a rough guide.

- That you do not perform any UTXO management, and will want to move all of your BTC in one go.

What are UTXOs?

Every time you buy bitcoin, it creates a separate piece of bitcoin (called a UTXO) in your wallet. When you want to spend your bitcoin later, you need to pay a network fee for each UTXO you use. If the network fees are high and you have many small UTXOs, it might cost more in fees than some of your bitcoin is worth!
